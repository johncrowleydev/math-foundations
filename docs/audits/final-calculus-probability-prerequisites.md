# Final Calculus and Probability & Statistics prerequisite/progression audit

Inspected 2026-09-20 against `3719ac2` and the mathematical prompt repairs in [the companion report](final-calculus-probability-mathematics.md). This is an instructional-sequence audit, separate from Review activation or coverage counts.

All 44 instructional lessons, both introductions, and their exercise order were read. No missing prerequisite support or substantive sequencing concern was found when following the declared complete path: Discrete Mathematics, Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability & Statistics. The Calculus introduction explicitly assumes school algebra, functions, exponent/logarithm familiarity, basic trigonometry and radians. Probability & Statistics explicitly relies on the earlier subjects. This conclusion does not assert that either advanced subject is a standalone course for a learner without its stated prerequisites.

The table classifies the material assumed before each lesson. “Intentional prerequisite” identifies earlier curriculum or an explicit introductory prerequisite; “local refresher exists” means the lesson explains the relevant convention/technique before using it. Elementary substitution, signed arithmetic, fractions, polynomial expansion and solving simple linear equations are “acceptable common algebra” throughout. No Precalculus subject or extra Review cards are needed for the findings here.

## Clear pacing findings

**P1 — LOW: Calculus 16, `calculus-multivariable-functions`.** Exercises **24, 26, 28, 30, 32** repeat the same continuity proof for `cx²y/(x²+y²)` with c=−3,−1,1,2,4. Each uses `|cx²y/(x²+y²)|≤|c||y|`; after the first proof, the reasoning barely changes. Exercises **47 and 49** are exact duplicates of the saddle explanation for `x²−y²`. This is unnecessary repetition for a learner completing the entire bank, not a difficulty cliff. The introduction already treats practice as a bank rather than a requirement to finish every question in one sitting. Proposed bounded improvement: a local study note to select one coefficient proof initially and revisit others for retrieval, or later replace one duplicate with an independently supported transfer task while preserving IDs. No mechanical deletion or new cards were made in this verification pass.

**P2 — LOW: Calculus 19, `calculus-multivariable-extrema`.** Exact duplicate pairs are **22/28**, **24/30** (quadratic saddle classification), **42/44**, **46/50** (attainment on an open disk). The surrounding lesson does include nonquadratic inconclusive cases and global boundary comparisons, so there is no missing transfer category. Proposed bounded improvement: guide the learner to one representative from each repeated pair; any later content substitution should retain stable IDs and receive its own mathematical/source inspection. Left unchanged to avoid repurposing saved exercises during a hardening pass.

No BLOCKING, HIGH or MEDIUM prerequisite/pacing finding was established. Other repetition uses changed cases that matter mathematically, such as signs, endpoints, support, conditional versus unconditional quantities, or different data-generating assumptions. Exercise counts were not equalized.

## Limits of this result

These are mathematical and instructional judgments, not measured learner difficulty ratings. No completion-time or learning-outcome data was available. Proof exercises assume the proof methods from Discrete Mathematics; logarithms, basic trig and algebra remain intentional entry prerequisites. The report does not treat Review eligibility as proof that a learner has mastered those prerequisites. The newly repaired standalone question context is documented in the mathematical audit rather than misclassified as a missing subject prerequisite.
