## Selective changes and evidence boundaries

Five authored families add ten variants. Four families provide deterministic Boolean recognition in Quick; the estimator/estimate family requires actual short-text term production. Each has a separate recall objective. Existing computation and reasoning objectives, exercise IDs, and source digests are preserved. New activation uses only the concept of the teaching section where the vocabulary is introduced. The expectation probe activates in the continuous-expectation section, after the calculus prerequisite; it is not activated just by earlier discrete expectation work.

The source-of-truth changes are in the five existing authoring modules. Their normal subject generators were run. Semantic comparison confirmed every pre-existing review template and assessment fixture unchanged; formatting and ordering churn in otherwise unchanged generated files was discarded. Only the five new inspected review hashes were recorded.

## Source passages inspected on 2026-09-20

- OpenStax, _Calculus Volume 3_, [§4.3, definitions (4.12)–(4.13)](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-3/pages/4-3-partial-derivatives): one coordinate varies while the other stays fixed. Assignment `os3-4-3`.
- OpenStax, _Calculus Volume 3_, [§4.6, Gradient definition (4.39)](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-3/pages/4-6-directional-derivatives-and-the-gradient): ordered vector of partial derivatives, rather than their scalar sum. Assignment `os3-4-6`.
- Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong, _Mathematics for Machine Learning_, [§5.3, Definition 5.6, printed p.150 / PDF p.156](https://mml-book.github.io/book/mml-book.pdf#page=156): output rows, input columns, and rectangular Jacobians. Downloaded and inspected PDF pp.155–159 after the web extractor failed. Assignment `calculus-mml-jacobian`; retain the documented scalar-gradient orientation convention.
- Pishro-Nik, [§4.1.2, expectation and LOTUS formulas](https://www.probabilitycourse.com/chapter4/4_1_2_expected_val_variance.php): density-weighted accumulation, distinct from normalization. Assignment `ps-pn-4-1-2`. The new prompt explicitly requires a finite first absolute moment.
- Pishro-Nik, [§8.2.1, point-estimator notation](https://www.probabilitycourse.com/chapter8/8_2_1_evaluating_estimators.php) and [§8.2.3, first worked example and likelihood setup](https://www.probabilitycourse.com/chapter8/8_2_3_max_likelihood_estimation.php): random-data estimator versus an estimate from observed values. Assignments `ps-pn-8-2-1` and `ps-pn-8-2-3`; [§8.2.0](https://www.probabilitycourse.com/chapter8/8_2_0_point_estimation.php) additionally confirms the estimator definition.

## Intentional limits

No new generator, validator, lesson explanation, prerequisite subject, or deep proof pool was needed. Quick gaps for integrations, Jacobian construction, model setup, multistep estimation, and proofs remain intentional. The existing curriculum already contains gradient/least-squares, integrals/density, counting/probability, and linear-algebra/regression transfer; those were not copied into another deck. Further variants for isolated explanations and broader terminology retrieval should follow actual delayed-attempt evidence, which is currently unavailable for these subjects.
