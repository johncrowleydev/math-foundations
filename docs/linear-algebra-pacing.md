# Linear algebra pacing — 16 September 2026

The authored revision expands linear algebra from 10 to 12 instructional lessons by splitting the former bases/rank/inverses and projections/least-squares lessons. The reading-only **00 Introduction** remains unassessed. This records the authoring checks and integration contract; it does not replace the historical [September 13 correction audit](linear-algebra-audit.md) or claim a completed deployment.

Displayed numbering changes without renaming existing lesson slugs. The first split gives coordinates, basis extraction, and dimension their own lesson before rank, row/null spaces, determinants, and inverses. The second develops projection geometry, orthonormal directions, and Gram–Schmidt before constant fitting, a fully worked line fit, residual checks, and rank-deficient parameters. QR remains a contextual introduction.

Inline questions follow the teaching they require. For example, row-space tasks follow the row-space explanation; the projection uniqueness proof follows its Pythagorean argument. Least-squares question 38 supplies a line-fit transfer task with different data from worked question 37, alongside constant fitting and an exact-fit case. Original drills, proofs, counterexamples, and caveats remain available.

The revision retains the original 20 quick checks and adds four, for **24 quick checks: two per instructional lesson**. Existing checks move to their appropriate teaching sections while retaining their prompts, answers, and identities. The introduction has none.
