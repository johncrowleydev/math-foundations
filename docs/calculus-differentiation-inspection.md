# Calculus 01–08 authorship inspection

Inspected 2026-09-19. The explanations, examples, practice, review variants, glossary entries, and feedback in `scripts/authoring/calculus/01-*.mjs` through `08-*.mjs` are original teaching material. Citations identify mathematical background that was read and checked; they do not claim that OpenStax contains the particular authored wording, examples, or exercises.

Each lesson contains five teaching sections, fifty practice questions, fifteen separately authored review questions, and two quick checks. The introductions plus connected teaching prose are approximately 1,500–1,600 whitespace-delimited words per lesson. The eight modules provide seventy-five glossary entries. Question counts are descriptive, not validation thresholds; no percentage gate or percentage-reporting mechanism was added.

The modules use ordinary real-valued calculus. Fractional-power exercises specify positive domains unless a real odd-root convention is explicitly stated. Inverse trigonometric outputs and angle rates are in radians. An infinite limit is distinguished from a finite ordinary derivative. Critical inputs belong to the original domain; missing inputs and feasible endpoints are treated separately. These conventions were checked against the supporting passages and made explicit where necessary.

## Independent mathematical verification

Solutions were calculated while authoring from the stated definitions, identities, and differentiation rules. An additional independent pass recomputed thirty representative values using Python's standard-library `Fraction` arithmetic, without reading assessment expected-value fields. It covered:

- Polynomial and rational limit substitution; a linear epsilon/delta bound; cubic and square secant slopes; the reciprocal derivative quotient; tangent point substitution; marginal cost.
- Product/quotient table calculations, an inverse-cubic derivative, an implicit ellipse slope, logarithm composition, and signed relative rate.
- Competing rectangle rates, a cone's similarity coefficient, ladder and separation rates, angular rate, linear estimates, exact square increments, and differentials.
- MVT slope matching, a derivative-based change bound, constrained profit, box volume, positive-domain cost stationarity, and a squared-distance minimum.

Examples of independent results: the cone with radius `h/3` has volume `pi*h^3/27`; the ladder with horizontal distance `5`, vertical height `12`, and outward rate `3` has top rate `-5/4`; the exact square increment from `5` to `5.02` is `501/2500`, while its differential estimate is `1/5`. The closest point on `y=3x` to `(5,0)` is `(1/2,3/2)`, with squared distance `45/2`.

The cubic derivative sign chart and the full endpoint/critical-value comparison for `x^3-3x` on `[-2,3]` were checked independently. The minimum value is `-2` at two candidates and the maximum is `18` at the right endpoint. Twelve central-difference evaluations at ordinary inputs provided additional sanity checks for the derivatives of `x*exp(x)`, `ln(1+exp(x))`, and the logistic expression. Those numerical checks are error detection only, not proofs of symbolic equivalence or replacements for the displayed derivations.
